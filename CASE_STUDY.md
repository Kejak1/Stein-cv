# Strategic Case Study: Operational Architecture & Unit Economics
**Author**: Steinley Goh  
**Focus Areas**: Business Intelligence Analytics, Multi-Dealer Routing Infrastructure, Systems Architecture, CRM Integrity

---

## 🏛️ Executive Summary
This case study outlines the end-to-end mapping, prototyping, and deployment of operational tracking infrastructures engineered to eliminate manual data entry, enforce regional data integrity, and provide granular financial visibility. Leveraging native prototyping tools (**Figma**) and rigorous functional requirements documentation (**Google Docs**), complex messaging pipelines and unit-economic reporting models were established to drive precise decision-making impact on business.

---

## ⚡ Section 1: Multi-Dealer WhatsApp Routing & Distribution Engine

### **The Architectural Infrastructure**
Engineered a comprehensive lead generation pipeline linking the **Official Yadea WhatsApp Business API** (Meta) directly to a centralized **Mekari Qontak** routing engine via real-time webhooks. This architecture completely bypassed manual regional data collection, enabling automated ingestion and assignment at scale.

### **Intelligent Chatbot & Location Mapping**
* Designed automated screening chatbots to capture initial customer intent and geographic coordinates.
* Programmed routing logic to instantly evaluate customer location and assign incoming inquiries to the **three nearest authorized dealer centers**.

### **Competition Mode Mechanics ("First-to-Respond")**
To maximize response speed and ensure absolute fairness across dealer distributions, a native **Competition Mode** workflow was implemented:
* Leads are broadcasted simultaneously to targeted dealer queues.
* Dealers must actively click to "claim" the lead inside their WhatsApp client to win exclusive ownership of the conversation.
* Full conversation logs and conversion timelines are synchronized back to central CRM databases automatically.

## 🛡️ Section 2: CRM Business Logic & Automated Fraud Prevention

### **Data Integrity Enforcement**
Operating across **110+ individual dealer accounts**, automated validation loops were scripted to maintain system-level accuracy and eliminate manual audit dependencies:
* **Duplicate Detection**: Configured asynchronous database triggers to scan and flag duplicate hardware serial numbers submitted by regional points instantly.
* **Pricing Audits**: Implemented operational threshold checks to detect regional pricing violations in real time, securing corporate warranty compliance and eliminating commission leakage.

### **Escalation Tiers (Mitigating Alert Fatigue)**
To prevent notification blindness among operational leaders, customized alert pipelines were segmented into three priority tiers:
1. **Critical Alerts**: Real-time push notifications delivered natively via WhatsApp for urgent workflow blocks.
2. **Warning Feeds**: In-app operational dashboards tracking ongoing data validation discrepancies.
3. **Info Digests**: Automated asynchronous email recaps outlining regional metrics for executive review.

## 📊 Section 3: Unit Economics & Decision-Making Visualizations

### **The Philosophy of Dashboards**
Data visualization serves a singular, highly focused objective: transforming highly complex data streams into clean interfaces that give business leadership absolute clarity to make precise decisions. 

### **The 10 Core Metrics Engine**
Interactive reporting matrices built inside **Feishu Base** and **Google Looker Studio** model exactly how operational marketing spend translates into finalized sell-through performance. The analytics engine synthesizes 10 distinct variables alongside granular **Lead Source ROI comparisons** (benchmarking TikTok campaigns, Google Ads, and physical retail walk-ins):
1. **Total Event Spend**: Overall corporate capital deployed per initiative, aggregated across monthly intervals.
2. **Dealer Support Cost**: Capital subsidies allocated directly from headquarters to support active promotional operations.
3. **Baseline Dealer Cost**: Fixed operational overhead mapped to individual retail points.
4. **Out-of-Pocket Dealer Spend**: Independent capital spent directly by local partners to run marketing activities.
5. **Average Cost per Activity**: Direct resource-efficiency tracking for specific promotional themes.
6. **Average Cost per Day**: Ongoing operational burn rates across active campaign lifecycles.
7. **Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) Mechanics**: Monthly absolute CPL tracking, paired with dynamic daily trendlines and visual charts to monitor acquisition velocity in real time.
8. **Total Activity Volume**: Absolute numerical tracking of promotional pushes launched.
9. **Active Duration Days**: Combined regional operational footprint measured in cumulative campaign days.
10. **Verified Outcomes**: Absolute monthly lead generation quotas verified against finalized retail unit sales.
