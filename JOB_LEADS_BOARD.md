## 3. The "Secret" LinkedIn Search Hacks

Don't just search "jobs" on LinkedIn. Type these exact phrases into the **LinkedIn Search Bar**, filter by **Posts** (not Jobs), and sort by **Past Week**. This shows you founders and hiring managers posting about open roles *before* they hit the official job boards.

*   `"hiring" AND "marketing operations" AND "remote"`
*   `"looking for" AND "revenue operations" AND "remote"`
*   `"hiring" AND "data analyst" AND "looker studio" AND "remote"`
